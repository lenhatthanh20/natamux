The sidebar shows subtasks and agent shells under their task, a task that waits for you says so once in each area, and the keyboard goes straight to the first agent when a task opens. If you have v0.1.2 or later, the app updates itself to this version.

Guide: https://natamux.github.io/docs/

## Dashboard

- Open subtasks sit under their parent task in the sidebar. A task with open subtasks, or with stages that run side by side, has a line under it such as "2 open subtasks · 2 parallel stages". Click it, or press `Right` and `Left` on the row, to show or hide its agent shells and subtasks. Click a shell to open the task's terminal on it. The line opens by itself while something under it works, waits or failed, and Natamux remembers what you fold. See [Sidebar](https://natamux.github.io/docs/guides/dashboard/#sidebar).
- The line under a workspace title shows the agent, the task id, the stage and its step (such as "Review 3/5"), the pull request, how many subtasks are done, and the size of the diff. Hover the id to see the branch. A blue dot marks a change you have not seen yet.
- A task that waits for you says so once in the sidebar, in **Needs you**, and once in its workspace, in the banner. The workspace row, the workspace header and the terminal pane no longer repeat it.
- The state is called **Needs your input** everywhere. When the agent ends its turn without `natamux done`, the banner says **The agent ended its turn. Reply in the terminal, or resume it.** See [Needs you](https://natamux.github.io/docs/guides/dashboard/#needs-you).

## Terminal

- A terminal pane has no amber bar or tint while its agent waits. When the workspace shows more than one agent pane, the waiting pane says **Needs your input** in its header. See [Type to the agent](https://natamux.github.io/docs/guides/terminal/#type-to-the-agent).

## Fixes

- Fix: when you opened a task with two shells side by side, the keyboard went to Shell 2 first and then jumped to Shell 1. Now Shell 1 gets the keyboard at once. A shell you pick with **Answer in terminal** or **Jump to a workspace** gets it with no stop in Shell 1.

## Before you open it

This release is **not signed** with an Apple certificate.

- **macOS:** drag Natamux to Applications and open it once. macOS blocks it. Then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. If there is no such button, run:

  ```sh
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Natamux.app
  ```

An update from v0.1.2 or later does not need these steps.

See the [install guide](https://natamux.github.io/docs/getting-started/install/).
