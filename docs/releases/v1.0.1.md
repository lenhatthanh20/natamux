In the desktop app, an open task now shows its terminals in the main area, and its views stand on a rail at the right edge. This release also fixes the sidebar and Reopen agent. If you have v0.1.2 or later, the app updates itself to this version.

Guide: https://natamux.github.io/docs/

## Desktop app

- An open task has no tabs. Its terminals fill the main area. **Progress**, **Changes**, **History**, **Docs**, **Files** and **Details** are icons on a rail at the right edge of the window, and the rail stays when the details panel is closed. Click the picked view again to close the panel. See [In the desktop app](https://natamux.github.io/docs/guides/dashboard/#in-the-desktop-app).
- **Changes** lists the files and the commit box in the panel and shows the picked file's diff over the terminals. **Docs** lists the stage files in the panel and shows the picked one over the terminals. A file from **Files** opens over the terminals too. **Terminals**, or `Esc`, shows the terminals again, and `Mod+W` closes the open file.
- To add a terminal, click the split button in a terminal's header. On a finished task, **Back to wrap-up** in a terminal's header goes back to the wrap-up.
- In **Start a task**, the selects (**Context from**, **Skill**, **Project**, **Branch from** and **Agent**) sit on the row of their label and are as wide as their text. A long name is cut short.
- `Enter` starts a new line in **Notes**, in the notes of the edit form, in the send-back reason, in the commit message and in the **Ask agent** comment.

## Fixes

- In the sidebar, the buttons of a project's header followed its task count, and the rows under a task were not indented. Now the buttons sit on the right and the rows are indented again.
- After a task was finished, **Reopen agent** could show the ended Terminal 1. Now it shows the new terminal of the agent.

## Before you open it

This release is **not signed** with an Apple or Windows certificate.

- **macOS:** drag Natamux to Applications and open it once. macOS blocks it. Then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. If there is no such button, run:

  ```sh
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Natamux.app
  ```

- **Windows:** run `Natamux-win-x64.exe`. If SmartScreen shows **Windows protected your PC**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer needs no administrator rights.
- **Linux AppImage:** run `chmod +x Natamux-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, then start it. If it does not start, start it with `--appimage-extract-and-run`, or install the `.deb` instead. The updater needs `curl`.
- **Debian and Ubuntu:** run `sudo apt install ./Natamux-linux-amd64.deb`. The app is `/opt/Natamux/natamux-app`.

An update from v0.1.2 or later does not need these steps.

See the [install guide](https://natamux.github.io/docs/getting-started/install/).
